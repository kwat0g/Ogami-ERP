- Finished goods are in draft [PR #524](https://github.com/kwat0g/kwatog/pull/524) on stacked branch `feat/demo-finished-goods-master-data`. Eight existing product costs carry forward from the current product reference catalog; the other eight costs and all synthetic dimensions/tolerances are illustrative demo assumptions, not released customer specifications. Prices remain unset and products remain restricted pending the current BOM cost and customer pricing workflows.
- Production machines, molds, and asset register entries are in draft [PR #525](https://github.com/kwat0g/kwatog/pull/525) on `feat/demo-production-assets`. `production-assets` depends on both `organization` and `finished-product-catalog`, creates equipment through `MachineService`, `MoldService`, and `AssetService`, links operational records through `AssetService::associate`, and writes mold compatibility through `MoldService::syncCompatibility`. The current stack sizes this layer at small 4 machines / 16 molds / 3 auxiliary assets, demo 30 / 60 / 12, and full 36 / 96 / 12; small mode keeps one mold for each product so downstream process baselines cover all products. The current combined focused suite passes both asset scales and the inventory/product tests. Acquisition values and equipment specifications are marked synthetic estimates; see [production-assets.md](production-assets.md).
- BOMs and production routings are in draft [PR #526](https://github.com/kwat0g/kwatog/pull/526) on `feat/demo-bom-routings`, stacked on #525. `bom-routing-catalog` depends on `production-assets`, creates 16 active BOMs / 44 component rows and 16 active routings / 48 operations through `BomService` and `ProductionRoutingService`, and relies on the app's BOM cost service to publish standard-cost-plus-routing snapshots. Its focused test passed (1 test, 353 assertions) for cost warnings, units, cost sources, product/mold/machine compatibility, timestamps, and idempotence. The final combined focused run passed 13 tests / 435 assertions across the BOM/routing layer, both asset scales, earlier catalogs, command guards, and reset gate. Process quantities and shop rates are synthetic and documented in [bom-routing.md](bom-routing.md) and F-018.
- Fictional customer and vendor masters are in progress on `feat/demo-business-partners`, stacked on the BOM/routing branch. `business-partners` depends on `finished-product-catalog`; small mode creates 3 customers / 5 vendors, demo 10 / 18, and full 12 / 24. Customer codes come from `CustomerService` and the configured customer sequence; product access and synthetic warranty terms are created through its existing service path. Final focused tests pass (2 tests, 152 assertions), covering small and demo volumes, one inactive partner per type in demo scale, `.test` email subdomains, no tax IDs, controlled timestamps, product access, warranty options, and rerun idempotence. Supplier-to-item qualification links are deferred to the Purchasing supplier-listing workflow; customer price agreements are deferred to CRM's pricing workflow (F-019). Details are in [business-partners.md](business-partners.md).
