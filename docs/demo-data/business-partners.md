# Business partner catalog

`business-partners` is the Phase 3 customer and vendor reference layer. It depends on `finished-product-catalog` so customer catalog access and warranty choices reference the current CRM products.

| Scale | Customers | Suppliers |
|---|---:|---:|
| small | 3 | 3 |
| demo | 10 | 6 |
| full | 10 | 6 |

The customer set is the ten companies and plant locations supplied for the demo: three Mitsuba plants, Hiblow Philippines, Sanac Philippines, Yazaki EDS, Yazaki-Torres, YGP, Ogami Japan, and Honda Philippines. The supplier catalog is restricted to the six suppliers supplied by the user: Inabata Philippines Inc., Philippine Nakamura, Aikawa Philippines Inc., Junlec Printing Supply, Tanigawa Seisakusho, and Dyna Pack & Malinta. No other customer or supplier remains active at demo/full scale. Historical links prevent deletion, so prior counterparties are deleted through `CustomerService`/`VendorService` where allowed and otherwise deactivated while linked documents remain intact.

Customer codes are generated by `CustomerService` and the application's customer document sequence, using the requested `--as-of` year. Customer product access, customer-specific warranty options, and warranty terms are created in the same service call. Product sets are restricted by customer segment and collectively cover all 16 seeded finished goods. Warranty terms are visibly synthetic, illustrative demo terms; they are not legal offers or actual customer commitments. Product prices and agreements are left to the CRM pricing workflow, whose review notifications are tracked under F-019.

Suppliers are created and updated through `VendorService`. Customer and supplier tax-ID fields are left null: no real or invented government IDs are stored. Withholding classifications use the application's `WithholdingTaxType` enum; no rate is duplicated or supplied by the demo layer. Credit limits, payment terms, contact roles, and supplier addresses not provided by the user are synthetic demo details. Contact emails use Gmail-formatted addresses generated from distinct contact names; these are checked against employee logins. Phone values use a visibly synthetic `+63 000 555 ...` pattern and are not dialable. The demo runner fakes mail, broadcast, queue, and cache drivers.

Supplier-to-item qualification links are created by `SupplierListingLayer` through Purchasing's listing workflow. Inabata sources resins, colorants and process consumables; Philippine Nakamura and Aikawa source metal inserts; Aikawa, Junlec, Tanigawa and Dyna Pack source packaging. Maintenance spare parts remain unqualified because none of the six supplied suppliers is identified as a spare-parts source. Base selling prices and price agreements remain in CRM's price-review workflow.

The layer repairs mutable scalar master fields through `CustomerService` and `VendorService`. Customer product-access or warranty-definition drift is detected and fails with a reset-required message because the current customer service has a create path for those relations but no corresponding reusable update service. A reset followed by `demo:seed` is the supported way to apply changed relation definitions.

## Verification

`BusinessPartnerCatalogLayerTest` runs the small profile through the layer dependency graph and checks counts, active records, generated customer codes, safe email domains, empty tax IDs, working customer product access, warranty options, and a repeat seed without duplicate rows or relation drift. No production DB, legacy demo seeder, or full PHP test suite is used by this layer test.
