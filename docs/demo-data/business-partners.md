# Business partner catalog

`business-partners` is the Phase 3 customer and vendor reference layer. It depends on `finished-product-catalog` so customer catalog access and warranty choices reference the current CRM products.

| Scale | Customers | Vendors |
|---|---:|---:|
| small | 3 | 5 |
| demo | 10 | 18 |
| full | 12 | 24 |

All names are fictional, industry-shaped counterparties. The customer set spans illustrative automotive electronics, motion, motorcycle, and enclosure buyers. The vendor set covers polymers, colorants, packaging, metal inserts, industrial spares, tooling, calibration, logistics, controls, and maintenance supply. Addresses and map points are illustrative locations in Cavite, Laguna, Batangas, Rizal, and Metro Manila; they do not identify customer receiving facilities. Contact names use the layer's fixed-seed `en_PH` Faker stream.

Customer codes are generated by `CustomerService` and the application's customer document sequence, using the requested `--as-of` year. Customer product access, customer-specific warranty options, and warranty terms are created in the same service call. Product sets are restricted by customer segment and collectively cover all 16 seeded finished goods. Warranty terms are visibly synthetic, illustrative demo terms; they are not legal offers or actual customer commitments. Product prices and agreements are left to the CRM pricing workflow, whose review notifications are tracked under F-019.

Vendors are created and updated through `VendorService`. Customer and vendor tax-ID fields are left null: no real or invented government IDs are stored. Vendor withholding classifications use the application's `WithholdingTaxType` enum; no rate is duplicated or supplied by the demo layer. Payment terms, credit limits, contact roles, addresses, and shipping responsibility are illustrative commercial values, not Ogami policy. Phone values use a visibly synthetic `+63 000 555 ...` pattern and are not dialable. Emails use unique subdomains beneath the reserved `.test` TLD (`*.ogami-demo.test`). The demo runner fakes mail, broadcast, queue, and cache drivers.

The demo layer does not create supplier-to-item qualification rows. Supplier-listing review is part of the Purchasing process, so item sourcing links will be created with that later workflow so qualification state and its derived catalog-change event are represented together. It also leaves base selling prices and price agreements to CRM's price-review workflow; historical price changes must account for the direct notification path described in F-019. These are deliberate deferrals, not populated supplier or price catalogs.

The layer repairs mutable scalar master fields through `CustomerService` and `VendorService`. Customer product-access or warranty-definition drift is detected and fails with a reset-required message because the current customer service has a create path for those relations but no corresponding reusable update service. A reset followed by `demo:seed` is the supported way to apply changed relation definitions.

## Verification

`BusinessPartnerCatalogLayerTest` runs the small profile through the layer dependency graph and checks counts, active records, generated customer codes, safe email domains, empty tax IDs, working customer product access, warranty options, and a repeat seed without duplicate rows or relation drift. No production DB, legacy demo seeder, or full PHP test suite is used by this layer test.
